From the ground to everyday life

Our business

We mine across the metals and energy markets

Industrial assets on six continents produce copper, cobalt, nickel, zinc, ferroalloys and coal.

Then smelt and refine it

Concentrates become metal at our smelters and refineries, to the specification each customer needs.

Move it by sea, rail and truck

Commodities travel from where they are plentiful to where they are needed, stored and delivered on time.

Market it worldwide

We source from our own assets and third parties, and sell to automotive, steel, power and oil customers.

And bring it back

End-of-life electronics and batteries are recycled into copper, nickel, cobalt and precious metals.

Our 2024-2026 Climate Action Transition Plan targets at least a 15% reduction in Scope 1, 2 and 3 industrial emissions by the end of 2026, 25% by 2030 and 50% by 2035, against a restated 2019 baseline.
