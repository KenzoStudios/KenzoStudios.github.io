Who we are

Scale, diversity and a single purpose.

One of the world's largest globally diversified natural resource companies, headquartered in Baar, Switzerland.

Through the scale and diversity of our industrial and marketing businesses, we responsibly supply the commodities that are fundamental to the building blocks of life.

Net zero ambition

A holistic approach to carbon reduction.

Our long-term ambition is to achieve, with a supportive policy environment, net zero CO2e emissions by the end of 2050, covering Scope 1, 2 and 3. We report annually on our progress.
