About this concept

An unofficial redesign of glencore.com

This concept was made by Kenzo Studios purely to show what can be done with HTML, CSS and JavaScript: a periodic table that opens into a scene, a value chain that draws itself as you scroll, and an emissions trajectory that plots as you read.

Not affiliated with Glencore

Glencore, its name, text and photographs belong to Glencore. Nothing here is an official statement or investment information. Every section links to the original page on glencore.com.

A snapshot

Text, figures, news headlines and share prices were taken from glencore.com on 27 November 2026. Share prices are a static copy and not live. Additional industry photography is from Unsplash.

How it is built

Plain HTML and CSS with GSAP ScrollTrigger for the scroll mechanics and Lenis for smooth scrolling. Motion turns off when your device asks for reduced motion.
