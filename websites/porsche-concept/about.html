About this concept

This is an unofficial concept redesign of porsche.com by Kenzo Studios. It was made purely to show what can be done with HTML, CSS and JavaScript: scroll-driven film, colour that changes chapter by chapter, and a variant explorer that follows the page as you read it.

Not affiliated with Porsche

Porsche, the model names and every photograph and cut-out on these pages belong to Porsche AG. Nothing here is an offer and nothing can be bought or configured on this site. Every model links to its page on porsche.com.

A snapshot

Images, model lines and specifications were taken from porsche.com (international) on 26 November 2026. Figures such as 0 to 100 km/h, power and top speed are copied as Porsche listed them that day, and may have changed since. Where Porsche did not list a figure, the page shows n/a.

How it is built

Plain HTML and CSS with GSAP ScrollTrigger for the scroll mechanics and Lenis for smooth scrolling. Photographs load from Porsche's own image servers rather than being copied. Motion turns off when your device asks for reduced motion.
