HOW TO PLAY

THE IDEA

Take turns lobbing shells across a destructible hill. Set your angle and power, mind the wind, and knock the other tank down to zero. Blow the ground out from under someone and they fall — and falling hurts.

Every round pays out, and a direct hit on a tank pays $100 on the spot. Winners earn a lot more than losers. Spend it in the Armory on shells and gadgets, which you keep for good, and on upgrades, which only last the current match.

TIPS

  • Wind pushes your shell the whole flight — a small number matters at long range.
  • Shooting the ground under an enemy is often better than hitting the tank.
  • Dirt Bomb buries a low enemy; Earthquake flattens the hill blocking your shot.
  • Cheap shells still win games. Save up for one Nuke, not five Heavies.
  • There is no arc preview — walk your shots in, and remember what worked. Buy an Aim Assist if you want one turn of certainty.
  • Supply crates parachute in every few turns. Drive over one to grab it, or blow it open from range — whoever fired the shot gets the contents.
  • With three or more players the round leader carries a bounty: damage dealt to them pays double.
  • Short of friends? Set any player to CPU on the match screen.
