Iron FrontierLegion Rising

A pixel-art roguelite run-and-gun. Fight down through sector after sector of the Legion's territory, pick up and fuse new guns as you go, and take on a boss at the bottom of each zone. Die and you start a fresh run, keeping the intel you collected to unlock more for next time.

Zones
Ashen Zone, Black Jungle, The Drowned Works, Rust Spires, Iron Citadel
Modes
Full runs, a Daily Operation with a shared seed, and The Spiral (endless)
Extras
Weapon fusions and evolutions, a codex of enemies and lore, a trophy wall, rebindable keys, colour-blind palettes
Plays on
Keyboard and mouse, gamepad or touch screen
Play from the startA fresh save, beginning with the tutorial climb.
Play with everything unlockedSkips the tutorial and opens every upgrade, achievement and codex entry, so you can see the late game straight away.
