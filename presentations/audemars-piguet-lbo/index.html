From wholesale to owned retail: the margin is already captured

2015

Wholesale-led

Hundreds of authorised dealers controlled the client and kept the retail margin.

2018-2024

The retail pivot

Dealer network largely terminated. Boutiques and AP Houses (private, apartment-style client spaces) opened in key cities.

2025

Direct-to-client

76 points of sale, about 90% of sales mono-brand. No e-commerce: every watch is sold by appointment.

Margin

Retail margin captured, supporting an estimated 35% EBITDA margin versus a 22% industry average.

Allocation

AP controls who buys which reference, protecting secondary-market prices.

Growth is now organic

The one-off uplift from the retail switch is largely complete, so future growth relies on price, mix and AP House roll-out.

Brand reach

The May 2026 Swatch x AP "Royal Pop" pocket watches (from USD 400) widened awareness without diluting the core line.

Steel, priced like gold

Gérald Genta's octagonal bezel, eight hexagonal screws and integrated bracelet launched at about CHF 3,300, more than many gold dress watches. It created the luxury sports-watch category.

Fifty years on, the Royal Oak family is still the economic engine of the House and the reason demand outruns production.

Path to control: winning the families, not just the auction

WHAT THE FAMILIES WILL TEST
Independence

AP's identity rests on being the last family-owned Trinity house.

Le Brassus

Jobs, manufacturing and the Vallée de Joux community.

Long-term brand stewardship

Fear of volume push or a return to wholesale. Breitling's 2026 write-down shows the risk of over-levered watch LBOs.

OUR PROPOSED COMMITMENTS
A

30% family rollover with tag-along, a pre-emption right at exit and a family vice-chair.

B

Brand charter: head office and manufacturing stay in Le Brassus; no return to wholesale; production cap reviewed only by the board.

C

Conservative capital structure: 6.0x at entry, below 3.1x by FY2029, no dividend recaps in the first three years.

D

Management continuity: Ilaria Resta and the leadership team stay, with a 10% management incentive pool.
