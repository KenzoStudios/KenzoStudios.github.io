Multi-source metabolic dataset from CGM, breathalyser, and cardiac sensors. Inaccessible to any third party without equivalent hardware. Core value unit of the Digital Twin.

Clinical monitoring heritage confers health data credibility that consumer tech rivals lack, a critical advantage when users share sensitive biometric data.

Device purchase, activation prompt, twin population, habit formation, coach discovery, and course enrolment. A natural, intuitive progression.

SECTIONS 6 & 7

Operating Model & Governance

MIMS INC.
TECHNOLOGY ARCHITECTURE
GOVERNANCE FRAMEWORK
LAWS
  • GDPR Art. 9, HIPAA, EU MDR / FDA SaMD compliance required
  • CGM and MetaBreath data classified as special-category
  • Formal regulatory opinion required pre-launch
NORMS
  • Professional Code of Conduct - no diagnostic or pharmacological claims
  • Mandatory clinical escalation protocol
  • Annual credential re-verification and content review before courses go live
ARCHITECTURE
  • Cohort-restricted messaging and encrypted data at rest
  • Raw biometric data not visible beyond authorised coach views
  • Automated flagging of prohibited content in course submissions
MARKET MECHANISMS
  • Outcome-weighted ratings reward higher-quality coaches
  • Persistent low ratings trigger a structured review process
  • Platform Commitment Charter caps commission and guarantees advance notice of rule changes
